Interview Preparation Questions:
- "Tell me about yourself" – Prepare a concise introduction covering education, projects, and career goals.
- "What are your strengths and weaknesses?" – Focus on technical strengths (Python, ML, React) and your learning mindset.
- "Describe a challenging project and how you solved problems" – Refer to hackathons, college events, or technical projects.
- "Why are you interested in this role/company?" – Mention specific alignment with your skills and long-term goals.
- "How do you keep your technical skills updated?" – Talk about online courses, events, and ongoing learning.
- Technical exercises – Practice Python coding, SQL queries, and common algorithms/DS questions.
- For ML/Data Science – Prepare for basic EDA, regression/classification questions, and simple model evaluation.
