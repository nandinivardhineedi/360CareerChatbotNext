Job Application Tips:
- Tailor your resume for each role, highlighting relevant skills and experience.
- Use action verbs and quantifiable achievements wherever possible.
- Showcase project work, hackathon participation, and any open-source contributions.
- Connect with recruiters on LinkedIn, join relevant career groups, and actively seek referrals.
- For freshers, mention internships, college events, leadership roles, and technical certifications.
- Personalize your cover letter, addressing company values and role expectations.
- Always follow up on applications with a polite email or LinkedIn message.
